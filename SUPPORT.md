To request a direction, open a **Funded direction** issue containing the asset, exact network, public transaction hash, and one specific feature, test, documentation area, or project direction. The first issue claiming an unclaimed confirmed inbound hash receives operational request attribution for that transaction. This is not cryptographic proof of wallet ownership, and a hash cannot be reused.

A donation may direct a request. It does not purchase support, ownership, investment returns, a delivery deadline, or acceptance of unsafe, illegal, private, proprietary, or out-of-scope work. Never post a private key, seed phrase, signature, credential, or private information.
